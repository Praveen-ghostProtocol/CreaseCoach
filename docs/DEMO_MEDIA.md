# Comparison demo footage

The one-click demo uses two attempts from one net session, filmed from the same camera position with the same batter. Both are two-second excerpts from [LayG Traveller’s batting practice clip](https://www.pexels.com/video/intense-cricket-batting-practice-session-40800361/) under the [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Both use the same centre crop, resized to 768 × 696 at the source rate of 24 frames per second, with audio removed. The footage illustrates visual review. Ball feeds, technique outcomes and body dimensions are unverified.

| Demo clip | Source start | Backlift | Downswing | Follow-through |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Net attempt 1 | 1.75 s | 0.83 s | 0.96 s | 1.38 s |
| Net attempt 2 | 3.75 s | 0.58 s | 0.71 s | 1.17 s |

The editable phase markers were placed manually from consecutive frames. Marker times in the table are relative to the excerpt start.
