# Comparison demo footage

The app bundles two short, muted practice excerpts under the [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/). They are used to demonstrate aligned playback from differently framed videos. The creators and depicted athletes are not associated with CreaseCoach. The app has no verified anthropometric records for either athlete.

| Demo clip | Creator and source | Edits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Field practice | [Sandeep Singh · Man Playing Cricket on a Sports Field](https://www.pexels.com/video/man-playing-cricket-on-a-sports-field-15172075/) | Excerpt starts at 3.25 s of the source and lasts 4.5 s. |
| Net practice | [Shulabh Singh Chauhan · Cricketer Practicing in Net Cage with Bat](https://www.pexels.com/video/cricketer-practicing-in-net-cage-with-bat-35804207/) | Excerpt starts at 0.35 s of the source and lasts about 4.15 s. |

Both excerpts are resized to 540 × 960, encoded at 30 frames per second in H.264, and muted. Their editable markers were placed manually by reviewing the excerpts. Differences in view, frame capture, and any existing slow motion limit inferences about technique or speed.
